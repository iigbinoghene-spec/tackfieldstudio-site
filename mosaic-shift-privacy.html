Mosaic Shift privacy policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Mosaic Shift game ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by EICI LLC under the brand Tackfield Studio ("we", "us"). Contact: support@tackfieldstudio.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. There is no account to create.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your phone.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, and measures how the game is played through Google Analytics for Firebase. These Google services collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Information stored on your phone

Your levels, stars, scores and settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion) are stored on your phone so you can continue where you left off. We can't see this information. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Information collected by our partners

PartnerWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob (advertising)Your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.To show the optional rewarded ad, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. Ads may be personalised unless you opt out or decline consent.
Google Analytics for Firebase (measurement)An app-instance ID, device and app information, approximate country, and in-game events such as starting, winning or losing a level and whether an ad was offered or watched.To understand how far players get, whether they return, and where levels are too hard, so we can improve the game.
Google PlayCrash reports and install statistics, as set in your phone's Google settings.To find and fix crashes.

These partners process data under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Ads are optional. You only see an ad if you choose to watch one.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings.
  • Deletion: uninstall the game to delete data stored on your phone. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app-instance ID, email us with the subject "Delete my data".

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game. Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit how it is used. This includes rights under Canada's privacy laws, the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012, the EU and UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws. To use these rights, email support@tackfieldstudio.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Security and transfers

Information collected by Google services is sent over encrypted connections and may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States, under Google's safeguards.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
