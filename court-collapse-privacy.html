Court Collapse privacy policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Court Collapse game ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by EICI LLC under the brand Tackfield Studio ("we", "us"). Contact: support@tackfieldstudio.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. There is no account to create.
  • Your match history, unlocks and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • The game can show ads through Google AdMob (an ad after a finished match, and optional ads you choose to watch), and measures how the game is played through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config. These Google services collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Information stored on your phone

Your matches, stats, progress and settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion, controls and graphics options) are stored on your phone so you can continue where you left off. We can't see this information. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Information collected by our partners

PartnerWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob (advertising)Your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.To show ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. Ads may be personalised unless you opt out or decline consent.
Google Analytics for Firebase (measurement)An app-instance ID, device and app information, approximate country, and in-game events such as starting, finishing or quitting a match, and whether an ad was offered, shown or watched.To understand how players use the game, whether they return, and where the game is too hard or confusing, so we can improve it.
Firebase Remote Config (settings)An app-instance ID and basic device and app information.To deliver game settings, such as how often ads appear, and to switch features on or off without an app update.
Google PlayCrash reports and install statistics, as set in your phone's Google settings. If you choose to rate the game, Google Play's in-app review window handles your rating.To find and fix crashes and to collect ratings.

These partners process data under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Feedback you send us

If you use "Send feedback" in the game, it opens your email app with a draft to support@tackfieldstudio.com that includes the game version and basic device details to help us fix problems. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. We use what you send only to reply and to improve the game.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings.
  • Optional ads: ads that give you something in return are always your choice to watch.
  • Deletion: uninstall the game to delete data stored on your phone. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app-instance ID, email us with the subject "Delete my data".

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game. Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit how it is used. This includes rights under the EU and UK GDPR, Canada's privacy laws, Brazil's LGPD and US state privacy laws. To use these rights, email support@tackfieldstudio.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Security and transfers

Information collected by Google services is sent over encrypted connections and may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States, under Google's safeguards.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
