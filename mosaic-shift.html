Mosaic Shift

Place shapes, clear lines, and slide a whole row sideways when the board needs it.

What it is

  • 1500 levels across 15 regions, each one checked to be winnable.
  • Shift: slide a row around the board to line up a clear.
  • Stars for winning, for moves to spare, and for score.
  • Undo is free and unlimited. Hints are free.
  • Daily Endless: a new random board every day, unlocked after level 10.
  • Plays offline.

Fair play

Every player gets the same level on every attempt. Levels never get secretly easier or harder based on spending or ads. The game has no purchases. Any ad is optional and only offered when it can actually help you finish.
