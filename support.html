Support

Email support@tackfieldstudio.com. We read every message.

To help us fix a problem, please include

  • The game (for example Mosaic Shift) and the level number.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • What happened, and what you expected. A screenshot helps.

Deleting your data

Your progress is stored only on your phone; uninstalling the game removes it. To ask us to delete any data linked to your device by our analytics or ad partners, email us with the subject "Delete my data". See the privacy policy for details.
