Court Collapse

Arcade tennis where the court breaks under your shots. Swipe to aim, open holes on your opponent's side, and win the point when the ball lands in one.

What it is

  • Swipe anywhere on the court to aim and hit. Timing and placement decide the shot.
  • Collapse Tennis: tiles crack and open into holes as the rally goes on. Classic Tennis: the same rules on an intact court.
  • Eight athletes, each with their own style and signature shot.
  • Hard, clay and grass courts that play differently.
  • Arcade matches, full tennis scoring, an arcade ladder and free training drills.
  • Power-ups earned during the match, never sold.
  • Line-call challenges, match stats and point replay.
  • Plays offline.

Fair play

Every player gets the same game. Nothing in it can be bought. Power-ups and athlete skills are earned or built in, never sold. Ads never appear during a rally.
